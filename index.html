Technology decision support

Welcome to GPU-SHIFT

Have you ever wondered whether to buy a piece of technology now, wait, or consider something different? When prices and availability change, finding a sensible option can be difficult.

We’re exploring how psychology and mathematics can help you compare your choices. Tell us a little about how you make decisions, your circumstances, and what matters most to you. We’ll show you an illustrative suggestion based on the options you enter.

Prototype website: GPU-SHIFT is an experimental and unvalidated research demonstration. It does not predict future market prices or provide financial advice. Your answers are processed in this browser and are not sent to a server.
