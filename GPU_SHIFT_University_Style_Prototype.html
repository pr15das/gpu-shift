DECISION SUPPORT / PILOT INTERFACE

Technology decision assessment

Welcome to GPU-SHIFT! Have you ever found yourself unsure whether to buy a piece of technology now, wait for a better opportunity, or consider an alternative? With changing prices, limited availability, and so many options, making the right decision can sometimes feel overwhelming. That's where GPU-SHIFT comes in. We're exploring how psychology and mathematics can work together to make these decisions easier. By understanding your personal circumstances, what matters most to you, and how you respond to uncertainty, we'll help you compare your options and find a choice that best fits your needs and priorities. Simply answer a few questions, tell us what you're considering, and explore your personalised suggestion. There's no universally perfect decision, but there may be one that makes more sense for you. GPU-SHIFT is currently an experimental research prototype. Its recommendations are illustrative and have not yet been scientifically validated.
